Are meals and snacks included in the fees?

Please contact the nursery directly for the latest information about meals, snacks and what is included in each session.

What is your payment policy?

Fees are payable in advance. We will always explain payment arrangements clearly before your child starts.

How do I secure a place?

You can secure a place by contacting us, arranging a visit and completing the registration process.
